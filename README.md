## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Data Science Engineering student at ESPRIT**, focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Data science.

My interests lie at the intersection of intelligent systems, large language models, data science and real-world AI applications.

I enjoy going beyond model experimentation: understanding architectures, preparing and processing datasets, fine-tuning models, building pipelines and integrating AI capabilities into functional applications.

Currently, I'm particularly interested in:

- 🤖 **Agentic AI:** Autonomous agents, tool use, orchestration and intelligent workflows.
- 🧠 **Generative AI:** LLMs, Transformers, LoRA and QLoRA fine-tuning.
- 👁️ **Deep Learning:** Computer Vision, NLP and attention-based architectures.
- ⚙️ **Data Engineering:** Distributed processing, data pipelines and scalable architectures.
- 🚀 **Applied AI:** Developing practical solutions to real-world problems.

### My Engineering Philosophy

I believe building useful AI goes beyond training models.

It requires understanding data, designing reliable architectures, optimizing models, and delivering intelligent systems that solve real problems.

My goal is to bridge the gap between AI research and production-ready engineering.
